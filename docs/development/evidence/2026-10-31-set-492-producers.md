## Result shape, pipeline and policy

`packages/core/src/render-result.ts:15,61,124,207` defines ten statuses, optional reason/diagnostics/evidence/outputs/target/standard, normalizes and validates individual shape. `target` and `standardProfile` are mutually exclusive. The result contains a source unit and optional path, but no enumeration or proof of all meaningful source fields. `render-result-policy.ts:4–24` always blocks `failed`; `lossy` and `unsupported` block only under unsupported policy `error`; all other statuses, including `rendered`, `degraded`, `transformed`, `metadata_only`, `target_native`, `externally_managed`, and `intentionally_skipped`, are nonblocking. That is actual current policy, not an endorsement that every nonblocking result is loss-free.
