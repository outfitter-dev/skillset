## Bounded MCP proof identity qualification

At `0b76d642`, synthetic structured-event probes confirm simple server-name extraction and rejection of missing correlated results. These are D2 parser checks. Claude's [MCP permissions documentation](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions#MCP) describes dot-to-underscore normalization and plugin-scoped names; such observed provider representations are D3, while their relation to a particular configured source remains D4 unless independently established.

Names such as `my.server` versus `my_server`, plugin-prefixed names, server/tool names containing `__`, and identical names from two plugins cannot establish unique ownership from the tool-name regex alone. The greedy delimiter parser has ambiguous possible splits. Same target/capability/name subjects merge and retain source units; current projection hashes establish freshness, not that one call exercised every retained source. Unmatched names remain unverified/D5. These synthetic probes do not demonstrate an actual provider issuing a false runtime receipt, so no D6 result or runtime success is claimed.

This MCP identity question remains open under SET-433 until a focused follow-up is assigned. Its required outcome is a source-qualified, provider-evidenced relation before attributing proof to a configured/plugin server, with unknown ownership unverified and coverage for normalization, scope, delimiter and same-name collisions. SET-438 covers migration lifecycle and complete-scan readiness; it does not own runtime MCP proof identity. No blind reverse-normalization or accepted reduced-assurance exception is authorized by this inventory.
